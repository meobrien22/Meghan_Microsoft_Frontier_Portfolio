THE CHALLENGE

Make the important
thing visible.

Scientific computing, management commitments and delivery responsibilities need to form one coherent argument. An oral presentation must work as a story and as evaluable evidence.

THE CREATIVE APPROACH

01

Keep the mission visible.

Connect the technical approach to the people and scientific work it supports.

02

Create a visual grammar.

Use consistent diagrams, chapter structure and hierarchy so presenters and evaluators share the same map.

03

Give evidence a place.

Connect service delivery, team ownership and accountability rather than listing isolated capabilities.
