The human story
behind AI.

Give an unfamiliar technology a familiar starting point: the person using it.

CONTEXT

Self-directed concept · fictional company Asterra

MY CONTRIBUTION

Executive narrative, art direction & presentation design

THE ARTIFACT

21-slide executive presentation

Self-directed presentation concept for fictional company Asterra. This is not commissioned Microsoft or client work.

THE CHALLENGE

Make the important
thing visible.

AI can become a story about everything and mean very little to an executive audience. The task was to give a broad set of capabilities a clear, human center.

THE CREATIVE APPROACH

01

Start with someone.

Lead with an everyday need, then reveal what the technology makes possible.

02

Let the image carry meaning.

Use human imagery, a clear hierarchy and concise headlines to keep the audience oriented.

03

Build toward a decision.

Move from possibility to practical scenarios, connecting the story to work people recognize.

What the work
demonstrates.

A complete narrative arc that demonstrates executive storytelling, visual judgment and the ability to translate technology without losing its purpose.
